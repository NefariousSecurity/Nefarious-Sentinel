Sentinel Message Service is an optional Android SMS event helper for
Nefarious Sentinel, used only with a matching Sentinel Core and Controller.

The helper requires Android SMS receive permission to process incoming SMS
events. Trigger validation and action authority reside in Sentinel Core,
not in this helper. SMS-based wipe is an opt-in, potentially destructive
feature; it may irreversibly erase an Android device.

Do not activate destructive triggers on a phone with irreplaceable data.
Real incoming carrier SMS acceptance has not been fully verified for
the current release candidate. Installing through F-Droid does not grant
Device Owner authority.

The matching Core, Controller and Motion helper must retain the same
developer signing identity. Nefarious Sentinel is open-source AGPL-3.0,
derived in part from Oblivion V2 by Lethe Labs.
