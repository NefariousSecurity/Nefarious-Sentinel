Sentinel Motion Service is an optional background helper app for
Nefarious Sentinel's Snatch Protection functionality.

Install with the same-version Sentinel Core and Controller signed by the
same developer certificate. This helper cannot independently manage or
wipe a device. It sends motion events to the authorised Core.

Motion sensing and the resulting Lock or Reboot actions must be enabled
explicitly and depend on the Android permissions and administration mode.
An ordinary F-Droid installation does not grant Device Owner privileges.

Nefarious Sentinel is AGPL-3.0 and is derived in part from Oblivion V2 by
Lethe Labs. Release 1.0.0 is a prerelease.
